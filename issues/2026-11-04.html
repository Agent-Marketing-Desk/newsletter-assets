Trunk or Treat 2026

How We Marketed Trunk or Treat for KW Signature and KW Southwest

This October, in collaboration with each office's Agent Leadership Council (ALC), we promoted two Trunk or Treat events: KW Southwest in Sugar Land and KW Signature in Katy. Each office got event flyers, a social media campaign with Facebook and Instagram ads, a series of emails, and a recap video we produced from the night.

Every agent also got their own personalized flyer to share, almost 500 at KW Southwest and almost 400 at KW Signature. Over 2,000 attendees came out in Sugar Land and over 500 in Katy.
